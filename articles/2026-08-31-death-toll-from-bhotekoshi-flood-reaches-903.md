summary: The death toll from the catastrophic Bhotekoshi River flood in Nepal has risen to 903, according to government figures. The disaster, which struck the districts along the river, left 4,247 people still missing, including 592 foreign nationals. Rescue, search, and identification operations are ongoing as authorities struggle to locate survivors and recover bodies. The flood, caused by intense monsoon rains and landslides, has devastated communities, destroyed infrastructure, and prompted a large-scale humanitarian response. International aid agencies and neighboring countries have offered assistance, while the Nepalese government coordinates relief efforts and urges families of the missing to remain hopeful.
