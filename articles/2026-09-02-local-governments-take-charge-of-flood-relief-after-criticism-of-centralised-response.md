summary: After criticism of the central government's reliance on security agencies for flood relief, local authorities in Nepal have taken the lead. Six‑party committees have been set up in the districts of Rasuwa and Nuwakot to coordinate the distribution of aid and resources to affected communities. The move aims to improve responsiveness, ensure equitable assistance, and address concerns that the initial response was too centralized and slow. While the shift to local management is welcomed, officials acknowledge challenges in logistics and funding as the monsoon season continues.
