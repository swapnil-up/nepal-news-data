<p>Dharan, September 8 Bhat-Bhateni Super Store in Dharan will officially resume operations from Wednesday, Bhadra 24. Panu Paudel, chief operating officer of Bhat-Bhateni Supermarket, said the store located at Putali Line will resume regular services from Wednesday. The company said it remains committed to providing modern facilities and quality services for the convenience of customers. [&#8230;]</p>
