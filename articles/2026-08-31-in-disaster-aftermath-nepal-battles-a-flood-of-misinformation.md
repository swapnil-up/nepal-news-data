summary: In the wake of a recent disaster in Nepal, authorities and rescue teams are contending not only with physical damage but also a surge of misinformation on social media. Platforms are awash with AI‑generated videos, recycled footage, and graphic content that misrepresent the scale and nature of the tragedy. Government agencies, the Nepal Red Cross and other responders are urging the public to rely on official updates and are working with tech companies to curb the spread of false material, emphasizing the risk such misinformation poses to rescue operations and public safety.
