summary: A severe flood in the Bhotekoshi River swept through Nepal's Rasuwa border area, leaving hundreds of traders, drivers and customs workers missing after six days. Authorities are still unable to confirm the exact number of cargo containers lost, but estimates suggest billions of rupees worth of merchandise may have been washed away. Rescue and recovery efforts are ongoing, with officials coordinating with local agencies to locate the missing and assess the economic impact on cross‑border trade. The disaster has highlighted vulnerabilities in the region's infrastructure and raised concerns over the safety of workers and the continuity of trade flows.
