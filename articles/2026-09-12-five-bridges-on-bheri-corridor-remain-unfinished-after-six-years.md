summary: Six years after construction began, five bridges on the Bheri Corridor in western Nepal remain unfinished, stalled at foundations and half-built pillars. The project, intended to improve road access to the remote Dolpa district, was originally contracted for completion within three years. Delays have left the infrastructure incomplete, hindering connectivity and economic development for local communities. Officials from the Ministry of Physical Infrastructure and Transport have faced criticism over contract management and funding issues, while residents continue to rely on limited and unsafe routes.
