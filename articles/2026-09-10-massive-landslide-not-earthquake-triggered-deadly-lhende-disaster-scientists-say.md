Seismic data analysis disproved initial earthquake assumptions, highlighting the landslide as the primary trigger. The findings underscore the need for improved landslide monitoring and disaster preparedness in the Himalayan region.

<p>Kathmandu, September 10 The devastating flood and debris flow in the Lhende Khola area along Nepal’s border with Tibet was triggered by a massive landslide rather than an earthquake, according to an analysis of seismic data by an international team of scientists. The disaster has killed more than 1,300 people and left many others missing. [&#8230;]</p>
