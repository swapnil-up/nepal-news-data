<p>Kathmandu, September 9 The Special Court has ordered Veridos, the German company contracted to print Nepal’s passports, to be released on a regular date without requiring bail. A bench comprising Special Court members Narayan Paudel and Dilli Ratna Shrestha ordered that Veridos be released on a regular date without requiring it to post any bail [&#8230;]</p>
