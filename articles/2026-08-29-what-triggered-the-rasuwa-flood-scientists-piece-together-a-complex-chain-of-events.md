summary: Scientists have reconstructed the sequence of events that led to the catastrophic Rasuwa flood in Nepal. They explain that a blockage in the Bhotekoshi River, caused by landslides and debris, built up a large volume of water. When the natural dam failed, a sudden surge of water rushed downstream, overwhelming communities and infrastructure. The analysis highlights the complex interplay of geological and hydrological factors, while noting gaps in data that still leave some uncertainties about the precise triggers.
