summary: The Nepalese government is constructing a 12‑km bypass road on the opposite bank of the Trishuli River to replace the landslide‑prone Krishnabhir section of the Prithvi Highway, which remains closed. Officials say the temporary repair work is uncertain, prompting the accelerated upgrade to ensure connectivity between Kathmandu and the northern districts. The project, overseen by the Ministry of Physical Infrastructure and Transport, aims to restore trade and travel routes vital for local economies, while residents and transport operators await a definitive timeline for the highway’s full reopening.
