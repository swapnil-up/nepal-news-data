summary: The article examines how Nepal’s recent floods have left people with disabilities facing ongoing challenges beyond the immediate disaster. While many survivors struggled to find safe evacuation routes, those with mobility impairments encountered additional hurdles such as inaccessible shelters, lack of wheelchair assistance, and limited medical support. Humanitarian groups and government agencies are working to address these gaps, but systemic barriers—like inadequate infrastructure, insufficient disability‑friendly planning, and delayed aid distribution—persist. The piece highlights personal stories of resilience, calls for inclusive disaster response policies, and urges stronger coordination between NGOs, local authorities, and disability advocates to ensure that future emergencies do not repeat these shortcomings.
