summary: A barrier lake that formed on the Nepal‑China border after a massive ice‑rock avalanche last week has largely emptied due to a natural outflow, Chinese authorities said. The avalanche had blocked a mountain stream, creating a lake that raised concerns of a sudden flood downstream. Over the past days, water has drained away, reducing the immediate risk of a catastrophic breach. Both Chinese and Nepali officials are monitoring the situation and assessing any remaining hazards for nearby communities.
