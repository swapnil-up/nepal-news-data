summary: The main border crossing between Nepal and China at Rasuwa-Kerung has been destroyed, cutting off the primary all‑weather trade route. Alternative passages through the Himalayas are prone to floods, landslides and severe weather, leaving Nepalese traders uncertain about moving goods during the upcoming festival season. The loss threatens supply chains, raises prices for consumers, and puts pressure on the government to expedite repairs or find safer routes. Stakeholders fear that the disruption could hamper cross‑border commerce and affect the broader economy, especially as merchants scramble for contingency plans amid the approaching holidays.
