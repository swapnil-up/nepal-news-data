summary: Heavy torrential rain caused severe flooding in the Nalgad area of Jajarkot district, Nepal. The flood inundated the Bheri Corridor, blocking the Jajarkot‑Dolpa road and halting transport along this vital route. Numerous homes, shops and other structures were swept away or heavily damaged, displacing residents and disrupting local commerce. Relief efforts are being mobilised as authorities assess the extent of the damage and plan for reconstruction of the road and affected properties.
