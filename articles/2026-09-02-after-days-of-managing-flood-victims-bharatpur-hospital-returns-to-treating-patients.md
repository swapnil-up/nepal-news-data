summary: Bharatpur Hospital in Chitwan, Nepal, has resumed regular patient care after spending several days handling the aftermath of severe flooding. The hospital managed the recovery of 321 flood victims' bodies, and over two days buried 199 of them, which relieved the strain on its morgue. With the morgue pressure eased, the facility can now focus on treating living patients again, marking a return to normal operations following the disaster.
