summary: Heavy rains have triggered unprecedented flooding in Nepal, described by Foreign Minister Shishir Khanal as a "Himalayan Tsunami." The disaster has inundated mountainous terrain and buried sections of the country's hydropower tunnels, hampering rescue efforts by multinational teams. Khanal warned that the catastrophe is a direct result of climate change, a phenomenon Nepal did not cause, and urged the international community to consider legal climate compensation for the damage. The minister emphasized the need for swift global action and financial support to rebuild critical infrastructure and aid affected communities, positioning the event as both a humanitarian crisis and a climate justice issue.
