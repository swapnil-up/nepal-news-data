<p>Kathmandu, September 10 Nepal has sought more than Rs 3 billion ($20 million) in compensation from the international Loss and Damage Fund, arguing that the devastating Rasuwa floods were linked to climate change. Nepal sent a letter to the Fund for Responding to Loss and Damage under the UN climate change framework last week. Finance [&#8230;]</p>
