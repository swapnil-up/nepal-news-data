Temporary burial sites have been set up for victims as authorities grapple with the massive humanitarian crisis. The floods, triggered by heavy monsoon rains, have devastated large areas in both Nepal and bordering regions of China, destroying homes, infrastructure, and farmland. Rescue and relief efforts are ongoing, with the Nepalese government and international agencies coordinating aid, medical assistance, and search operations. The scale of the tragedy has prompted calls for increased disaster preparedness and support for affected communities.
