<p>Kathmandu, September 8 Prime Minister Balendra Shah will lead a 15-member Nepali delegation to the 81st session of the United Nations General Assembly in New York, according to sources at the Prime Minister’s Office. The delegation will include Prime Minister Shah, Foreign Minister Shishir Khanal and the Prime Minister’s Chief Adviser Kumar Ben, among others. [&#8230;]</p>
