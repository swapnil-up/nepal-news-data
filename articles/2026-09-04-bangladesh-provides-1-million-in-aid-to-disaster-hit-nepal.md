<p>Kathmandu, September 4 Bangladesh has provided US$1 million in financial assistance to Nepal to support relief, recovery and humanitarian efforts following the devastating floods and landslides that hit northern Nepal last week. The Bangladesh government has contributed the amount to Nepal’s Prime Minister’s Disaster Relief Fund, according to the Embassy of Bangladesh in Kathmandu. The [&#8230;]</p>
