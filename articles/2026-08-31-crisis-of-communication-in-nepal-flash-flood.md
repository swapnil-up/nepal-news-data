Victims reported delayed warnings, inadequate coordination between the Ministry of Home Affairs, the Nepal Red Cross Society, and regional meteorological services, leading to higher casualties and property loss. Experts argue that rivers ignore political borders, so warning systems must be integrated across the Himalayas, urging stronger cross‑border protocols with India and China. The article calls for immediate reforms in disaster management, investment in real‑time monitoring technology, and community‑level training to prevent future communication failures.
