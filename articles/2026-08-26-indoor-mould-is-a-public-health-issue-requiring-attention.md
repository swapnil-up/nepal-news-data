summary: A recent article highlights indoor mould as an emerging public health concern in Nepal. Prolonged exposure to mould spores can gradually poison indoor air and accumulate in the body, leading to respiratory problems, allergies, and other chronic illnesses. The piece calls for increased awareness, better ventilation standards, regular inspections, and coordinated action by health authorities and housing agencies to mitigate the risk. It stresses that addressing indoor mould requires both public education and policy interventions to protect citizens’ long‑term health.
