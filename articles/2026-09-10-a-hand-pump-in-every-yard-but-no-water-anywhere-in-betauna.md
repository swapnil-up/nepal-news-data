<p>Chandardevi Mukhiya, 45, of Betauna in Golbazar Municipality–12, Siraha, has a hand pump right in her courtyard. But for the past several years, no water has come out of it. Since her own pump stopped working, she and other locals have depended on a submersible pump installed at the house of her neighbour, Mohan Jha. [&#8230;]</p>
