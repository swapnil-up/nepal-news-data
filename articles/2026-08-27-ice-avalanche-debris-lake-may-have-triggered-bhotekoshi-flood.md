summary: Satellite imagery released by Chinese authorities shows that an ice avalanche and a debris lake blocked the Bhotekoshi River about 20 kilometres upstream of the Miteri Bridge. The blockage caused water to accumulate until a sudden breach released a powerful surge that swept downstream, resulting in severe flooding in the Bhotekoshi basin. Local officials are investigating the event, which has damaged infrastructure, displaced residents, and raised concerns about future glacial lake outburst floods in the Himalayan region.
