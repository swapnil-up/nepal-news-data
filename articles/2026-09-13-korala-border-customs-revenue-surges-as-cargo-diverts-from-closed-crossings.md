summary: Following the August floods that forced the closure of the Rasuwagadhi and Tatopani border crossings, cargo traffic has been rerouted through the Korala border. In just 15 days, customs at Korala collected about Rs1.5 billion, reflecting a sharp increase in trade volume. However, the diversion has caused transport costs to Kathmandu to soar, rising roughly 15‑fold. The surge highlights both the economic opportunity for the Korala crossing and the logistical challenges faced by traders due to the disrupted traditional routes.
