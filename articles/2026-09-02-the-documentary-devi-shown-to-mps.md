summary: The documentary "Devi", which chronicles sexual violence endured by victims during Nepal’s decade‑long Maoist conflict, was screened for members of Parliament. The film highlights the continued suffering of survivors and criticises the government's apathetic response to their demands for justice and reparations. By bringing the issue to lawmakers, the documentary seeks to pressure the state into acknowledging the atrocities and implementing concrete measures to support the victims, who feel abandoned by the political establishment.
