summary: A barrier lake formed by floodwaters at the confluence of the Chhochen Khola and Purepu Tsangpo rivers near Nepal's border with China has breached, prompting fresh flood concerns in the Rasuwa district. The Nepalese authorities are preparing aerial reconnaissance missions to assess the situation and plan possible evacuations for downstream villages. Officials warn that the sudden release of water could cause significant damage to settlements and infrastructure, and they are urging residents to stay alert and follow safety directives.
