summary: The Nepalese government plans to establish a 50‑hectare tiger sanctuary in Devnagar, located in the buffer zone of Chitwan National Park. The sanctuary will provide a natural habitat for problem tigers that have come into conflict with humans, aiming to reduce human‑wildlife clashes while preserving the species. The initiative reflects ongoing conservation efforts to protect Nepal’s tiger population and manage wildlife‑human interactions more sustainably.
