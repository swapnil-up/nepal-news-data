<p>Kathmandu, September 9 The Supreme Court has dismissed a writ petition filed against former Chief Justice Cholendra Shumsher Rana. With the Constitutional Bench’s ruling, the way has been cleared for Rana to receive pension benefits and other facilities. Rana, who retired while suspended following an impeachment motion, had not received pension benefits or other facilities. [&#8230;]</p>
