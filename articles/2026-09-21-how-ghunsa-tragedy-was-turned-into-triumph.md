summary: Twenty years after a helicopter crash in Ghunsa, Taplejung that claimed the lives of Nepal's leading conservationists, including Dr. Chandra Gurung, the community commemorates the tragedy by highlighting the lasting impact of their work. The memorial event honors their contributions to protecting the Himalaya's biodiversity and showcases how local forest management, community-based conservation projects, and eco‑tourism initiatives have flourished, turning a sorrowful loss into a catalyst for environmental stewardship. Officials, NGOs, and residents reflected on the progress made in wildlife protection, sustainable livelihoods, and the establishment of protected areas that were championed by the fallen experts, underscoring a narrative of resilience and hope.
