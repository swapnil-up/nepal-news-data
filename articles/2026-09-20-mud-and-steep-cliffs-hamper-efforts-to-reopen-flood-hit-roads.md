The districts of Rasuwa and Nuwakot remain isolated from each other, and the headquarters of Dhading district still lacks a direct road connection. Authorities are struggling to clear debris and stabilize slopes, delaying the reopening of critical transport links that are essential for relief supplies and local economies.
