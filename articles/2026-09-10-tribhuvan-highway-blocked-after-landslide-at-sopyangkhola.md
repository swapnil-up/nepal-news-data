<p>Kathmandu, September 10 The Tribhuvan Highway has been blocked due to a landslide. Police in Dhading say the highway connecting Kathmandu and Hetauda was blocked in both directions after a landslide struck Sopyangkhola in Thakre Rural Municipality-7, Dhading, at around 9 pm last night. According to police, continuous rainfall has made it difficult to clear [&#8230;]</p>
