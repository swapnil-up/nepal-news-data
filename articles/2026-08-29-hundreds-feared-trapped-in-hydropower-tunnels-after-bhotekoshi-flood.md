summary: Heavy rains caused the Bhotekoshi River to flood, inundating several hydropower tunnel sites along Nepal's Trishuli corridor. Hundreds of construction workers are feared trapped underground. The Nepali Army has begun rescue operations, managing to evacuate workers from some locations, but access to many sites remains difficult due to swollen waters and damaged infrastructure. Authorities are assessing the extent of the damage and continuing search efforts, while the flood raises concerns over the safety of ongoing hydro projects in the region.
