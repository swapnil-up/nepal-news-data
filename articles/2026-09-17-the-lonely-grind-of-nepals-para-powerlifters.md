Despite facing physical challenges and a lack of institutional support, they remain determined to represent Nepal at the upcoming 5th Asian Para Games. Their story highlights the broader neglect of para athletes in the country, with minimal funding, inadequate facilities, and limited recognition from sports authorities. Yet their perseverance underscores a growing desire for inclusion and the potential of disabled athletes to excel on the international stage if given proper resources.
