<p>We have often heard, and repeatedly experienced, that Nepal is highly susceptible to natural hazards. The reasons are largely clear. Geologically, the Himalaya is a relatively young mountain system, and its mountain-building process remains active. The continuing convergence of the Indian and Eurasian tectonic plates produces uplift, seismic activity and ongoing changes in the terrain. [&#8230;]</p>
