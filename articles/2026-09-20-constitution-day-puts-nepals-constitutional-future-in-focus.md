Debates focus on issues such as federal restructuring, minority rights, and the balance of power between the executive and legislature. The discourse highlights both consensus on the charter's core values and deep divisions over how to adapt it to evolving political realities, underscoring the constitutional future as a central theme in Nepal's governance agenda.
