summary: The Nepalese government has allocated Rs675 million to fifteen local units affected by the Bhotekoshi flood. The funds are earmarked for search, rescue, and immediate relief operations in the districts of Rasuwa, Nuwakot, Dhading, Gorkha, and Tanahun. This release follows earlier disbursements to the most severely impacted districts, underscoring the government's ongoing response to the flood disaster.

The allocation will fund search, rescue and immediate relief in local units across Rasuwa, Nuwakot, Dhading, Gorkha and Tanahun, with the government having already released additional funds to the worst-hit districts.
