summary: The article highlights Nepal's struggle to identify flood victims due to a severely limited DNA analysis capacity. With only about 20% of bodies recovered from recent monsoon floods, families await identification, but the country's forensic laboratories lack the equipment, trained personnel, and funding needed for large‑scale DNA profiling. Government officials, including the Prime Minister and the Minister of Home Affairs, acknowledge the shortfall and have appealed for international assistance and increased budget allocations. NGOs and the Nepal Red Cross are also stepping in, but the bottleneck threatens timely closure for grieving families and hampers disaster response efforts.
