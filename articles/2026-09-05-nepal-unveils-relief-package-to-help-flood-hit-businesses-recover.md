summary: The Nepalese government announced a comprehensive relief package aimed at helping businesses devastated by the recent Bhotekoshi floods. The measures include customs and tax exemptions, restructuring of existing loans, and the provision of cheaper credit facilities. The package is intended to accelerate recovery, preserve jobs, and stabilize local economies in the flood‑affected districts. Officials say the assistance will be coordinated through the Ministry of Finance and relevant financial institutions, with priority given to small and medium enterprises that suffered the greatest losses.
