summary: The planned unveiling of a Gen Z memorial in Kathmandu has been postponed after families of the commemorated children complained that the statues do not resemble their loved ones. The families argued that the likenesses are inaccurate and disrespectful, prompting organizers to halt the ceremony. Sculptors involved in the project responded that they were pressured to complete the works quickly and were forced to display unfinished pieces, which they say contributed to the mismatches. The controversy has sparked debate over artistic standards, community consultation, and the handling of public memorial projects in Nepal.
