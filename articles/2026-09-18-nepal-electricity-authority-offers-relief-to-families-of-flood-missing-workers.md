summary: The Nepal Electricity Authority (NEA) announced financial assistance for families of employees who went missing during recent floods. A one-time payment of Rs 50,000 will be given to families of missing workers, while relatives of permanent staff will receive half of the employee's starting salary for up to six months. The relief aims to support affected households while the NEA assesses the broader impact of the disaster on its workforce.
