summary: The Nepalese government has allocated Rs675 million to fifteen local units affected by the Bhotekoshi River flood. The funds are earmarked for search and rescue operations as well as immediate relief measures in the districts of Rasuwa, Nuwakot, Dhading, Gorkha and Tanahun. This disbursement follows earlier releases of additional aid to the most severely impacted districts, underscoring the government's ongoing response to the flood emergency.

The allocation will fund search, rescue and immediate relief in local units across Rasuwa, Nuwakot, Dhading, Gorkha and Tanahun, with the government having already released additional funds to the worst-hit districts.
