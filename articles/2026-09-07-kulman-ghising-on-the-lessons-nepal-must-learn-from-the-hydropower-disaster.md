summary: Former National Electricity Authority chief Kulman Ghising warned that Nepal must draw critical lessons from the recent Bhotekoshi floods that devastated several hydropower projects. He highlighted the extensive damage to infrastructure, the challenges faced during rescue operations, and the urgent need for stricter design standards and safety protocols in the hydropower sector. Ghising urged policymakers, developers, and regulators to adopt comprehensive reforms, improve early‑warning systems, and ensure that future projects are built to withstand extreme weather events. The interview stresses that learning from this disaster is essential to safeguard Nepal’s energy security and protect communities downstream.
