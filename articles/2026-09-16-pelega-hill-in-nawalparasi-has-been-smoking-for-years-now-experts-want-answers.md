summary: Residents of Nawalparasi district have long observed smoke rising from various spots on Pelega Hill, but the phenomenon has intensified in recent months, prompting concern among locals. The persistent emissions have raised questions about possible underground fires, gas leaks, or other environmental hazards. Experts from the Nepalese Ministry of Forests and Environment, as well as local universities, have been called to investigate the source and assess health risks for nearby communities. Authorities are under pressure to provide answers and mitigate any potential danger to the region’s population and ecosystem.
