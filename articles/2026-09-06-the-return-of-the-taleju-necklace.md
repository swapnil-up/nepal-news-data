summary: After more than five years of diplomatic and legal efforts, Nepal is set to receive the historic Taleju necklace and several other artefacts that were taken abroad decades ago. The necklace, a prized symbol of the Taleju deity and the country's royal heritage, is being returned through cooperation between Nepalese authorities and foreign institutions. The repatriation is hailed as a significant step in preserving Nepal’s cultural legacy and underscores ongoing campaigns to bring back national treasures.
