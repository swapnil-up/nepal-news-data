summary: After more than five years of diplomatic and legal efforts, Nepal is set to receive the historic Taleju necklace and several other cultural artefacts that were taken abroad decades ago. The necklace, a sacred object associated with the Taleju deity and the Kathmandu Valley's royal heritage, has been the focus of a sustained campaign by Nepalese officials, heritage activists, and international partners to secure its return. The successful negotiations highlight growing global attention to cultural restitution and are expected to boost national pride and tourism, while also prompting discussions on the preservation of Nepal's remaining heritage collections.
