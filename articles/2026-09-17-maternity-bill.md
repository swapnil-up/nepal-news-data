The proposed changes aim to strengthen legal protections for pregnant women and new mothers, potentially expanding maternity leave, improving workplace accommodations, and enhancing access to reproductive health services. The draft will be presented to Parliament for discussion and approval, signaling the government's continued focus on women's health and gender equity.
